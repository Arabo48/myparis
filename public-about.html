About This Community

MY TOWN DIGITAL & TECH COMMUNITY exists to discover, showcase, and connect young people building digital and technology skills in our town — developers, designers, data professionals, creators, and entrepreneurs.

Every member gets a professional profile to display their skills, projects, and services. Organizations and mentors can find real, verified talent. Members can find opportunities, events, and mentors to help them grow.
